Our story · Since 1926

One address, one hundred years of taste.

Hollis & Rowe opened as a linen shop on Madison Avenue in 1926. A century later we are eight floors, three hundred ateliers and the same rule: a buyer must hold every piece before it reaches you.

A century on Madison

Four chapters

1926

 

A linen shop

Eleanor Hollis and Arthur Rowe open a 600 sq ft shop at 712 Madison Avenue.

1954

 

Eight floors

The shop becomes a department store. The ninth floor opens as a stylists' studio.

1989

 

The atelier program

We begin buying directly from 40 independent workshops across Europe and Japan.

2026

 

One hundred years

Three hundred ateliers, 12,000 pieces, and white-glove delivery to all 50 states.

The buying rule

Nothing is listed until a buyer has held it

Our fourteen buying directors travel eleven months a year. They visit every workshop, test every seam and sign off every piece. It is slower, and more expensive. It is why our return rate is under four percent.
