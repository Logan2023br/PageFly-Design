Frequently asked

Before you write

Where is my order?

Tracking is sent by email as soon as your order leaves our New Jersey atelier, usually within one business day. White-glove deliveries are scheduled by phone.

How do returns work?

Within 60 days, in original condition. Request a return from your account and we arrange a doorstep collection — complimentary for orders under $1,500.

Can I book a stylist?

Yes, complimentary. Choose video or in store; your stylist prepares a selection before you meet.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Every order can be wrapped in our linen gift box with a handwritten card, at no charge.
