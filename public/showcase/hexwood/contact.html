Before you write

The usual suspects

Will my order arrive before Halloween?

Order by Oct 27 (standard) or Oct 29 (express) and yes, guaranteed. Or choose store pickup — ready in 2 hours, until 8pm on the 31st.

How do size swaps work?

Request in your account; we ship the new size immediately and send a prepaid label for the old one. Free until Nov 3.

Do you do bulk orders for schools or offices?

Yes — 25+ items get 15% off and a dedicated rep. Email bulk@hexwood.com with your date and headcount.

Can I return decor after Halloween?

Unopened until Nov 15. Opened decor exchanges for store credit.
