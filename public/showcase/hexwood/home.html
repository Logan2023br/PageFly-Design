What if the costume doesn't fit?

Free size swap until Nov 3 — we ship the new size the day we get your request, before the return arrives.

Are kids' costumes flame-resistant?

Every Hexwood kids' costume meets CPSIA and 16 CFR 1610 flammability standards. Certificates on each product page.

Do you ship to Canada?

Yes — but the Halloween cut-off is Oct 20 for standard, Oct 24 for express.

Can I return decor after Halloween?

Unopened, yes — until Nov 15. Opened decor can be exchanged for store credit.

Is the candy allergy-labeled?

Every bulk bag lists allergens and we flag nut-free, gluten-free and vegan options with a green tag.
