The true story · Tucson, Arizona

It started at a drive-in

In 1978 Rosa Delgado ran the concession stand at the Starlite Drive-In. Every October she sold rubber masks next to the popcorn. They outsold the popcorn. Forty-eight Halloweens later, we still ship from the same town.
