The perfect pour-over in five minutes

One recipe, four tools and the three mistakes that ruin most cups.

6 min read · By Theo Lind, Quality Lead

Pour-over rewards precision. Weigh everything, time everything — then trust your tongue.

What you need

15g freshly ground coffee

250g water at 94°C

A dripper and paper filter

A scale and timer

The bloom

Pour 40g of water and wait 40 seconds. Fresh coffee releases CO₂ — the bloom lets water extract evenly afterwards.

“Fresh beans bloom. Stale beans just sit there.”

Medium-fine grind, like table salt.

The pour

Pour in slow spirals to 250g by 1:45. Total brew time should finish around 3:00.

Troubleshooting

Sour? Grind finer. Bitter? Grind coarser. Change one variable at a time.
