Subscription help

Most answers are right here.

How does the subscription work?

Pick your coffee, size and frequency. We roast on Monday and ship within 48 hours. Skip, swap or cancel anytime.

How fresh is the coffee?

Every bag is roasted to order and ships within 2 days of roasting, with the roast date printed on the bag.

Which grind should I choose?

Whole bean keeps longest. Otherwise choose the grind that matches your brewer — we'll tune it perfectly.

Is your coffee ethically sourced?

We pay an average of 2.4x Fairtrade minimum and visit every farm we buy from.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — to 30 countries. Free shipping on all subscriptions.
