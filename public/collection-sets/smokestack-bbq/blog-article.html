The easiest way to perfect ribs, every single time.

The recipe

3-2-1 spare ribs

6 hrs

Serves 4

Easy

01

3 hours

Smoke unwrapped at 225°F.

02

2 hours

Wrap with apple juice.

03

1 hour

Unwrap, sauce and set.

Why 3-2-1 works

Smoke builds bark, the wrap tenderizes, and the final hour sets the glaze.

“If you're looking, you ain't cooking.”

Pit tip

Remove the membrane on the back of the ribs for better smoke penetration.
