How much protein do you really need?

6 min read · Dr. Patel, Sports Nutritionist

The short answer: 1.6–2.2g per kilogram of bodyweight per day. The long answer is more interesting.

The science

A 2018 meta-analysis of 49 studies found muscle gains plateau around 1.6g/kg/day — with some lifters benefiting up to 2.2g.

“Total daily protein beats perfect timing.”

Timing myths

The 'anabolic window' is wider than you think. Hitting your daily total matters far more than the 30 minutes after training.

Spread protein across 3–5 meals.

Coach's tip

Aim for 30–40g protein per meal across 4 meals. Simple and effective.

80kg lifter → 128–176g per day

60kg lifter → 96–132g per day

Older athletes → aim higher
