5 things we learned from 200 episodes about work

7 min read · Maya Reyes

After 200 conversations with founders, nurses, CEOs and interns, a few lessons kept coming up — over and over again.

1. Burnout is structural

It's rarely about individual resilience. It's about workloads, unclear goals and broken incentives.

“You can't self-care your way out of a bad system.”

2. Boundaries beat balance

Balance is a feeling. Boundaries are a practice — and a negotiation.
