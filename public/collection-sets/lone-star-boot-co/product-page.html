FAQ

Fit & care

How do western boots fit?

Snug across the instep, with a slight heel slip that disappears after break-in. Most customers take their usual US size.

Are they handmade?

Every pair is cut, stitched and lasted by hand in El Paso, Texas — 180 steps per boot.

Free shipping and returns?

Free shipping both ways in the US, and free exchanges within 60 days.

Can boots be resoled?

Yes. Our Goodyear-welted boots can be resoled for life at our El Paso shop.

How should I care for them?

Brush after wear, condition every 3 months, and use cedar boot trees.
