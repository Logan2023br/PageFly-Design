Hạ Long Bay: the honest guide

9 min read · Updated March 2026

Hạ Long Bay is stunning — and stunningly crowded. Here's how to see it without the crowds, the tourist traps or the overpriced cruises.

When to go

March–May and September–November: calm seas, fewer boats, softer light.

Choosing a cruise

Pick 2 nights over 1

Choose Lan Ha Bay routes for fewer boats

Look for kayaking included

Avoid 'party' boats

“Wake up early. At 5:30am the bay belongs to you.”

What it costs

I spent $186 for 3 days including a mid-range 2-night cruise, transfers and food.

Insider tip

Book directly with operators in Cát Bà town — often 30% cheaper than online.
